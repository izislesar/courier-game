# Courier 404 — Product Vision

## One-sentence premise

Courier 404 is a first-person urban life and delivery immersive simulation about a young courier trying to live an ordinary life while discovering a hidden, far more profitable and dangerous anonymous delivery economy.

## Core fantasy

The player should feel that they inhabit a believable contemporary city rather than select missions from a menu.

They:
- wake up at home;
- check work;
- eat;
- travel;
- drive;
- pick up physical cargo;
- deliver it;
- spend money;
- maintain relationships;
- deal with consequences;
- decide how deeply to enter the anonymous grey/dark market.

The ordinary economy must remain viable. The dangerous economy is a temptation, not a mandatory morality branch.

## Product pillars

### 1. Mundane life is gameplay

Ordinary actions are not filler if they are tactile, fast and consequential.

Examples:
- getting food;
- fueling the car;
- walking through the apartment;
- receiving a message;
- loading cargo;
- driving home.

Routine should establish familiarity with the world without forcing constant maintenance.

### 2. Physical interaction beats abstract UI

The player should perform meaningful actions in the world.

The phone is the principal digital interface, but accepting a job must lead to physical movement and manipulation.

### 3. Systemic consequences beat moral-choice popups

Do not ask:

> Be good or evil?

Instead create situations where choices consume time, money, safety or trust.

Example:
- a high-paying anonymous pickup appears shortly before a planned evening with the girlfriend;
- the player decides what to do;
- the simulation records the actual consequence.

### 4. The city has two economies

Ordinary:
- food;
- parcels;
- documents;
- retail deliveries.

Hidden:
- anonymous sender;
- unusually high payout;
- changing destination;
- suspicious drop;
- risky time window.

The transition between them should feel gradual.

### 5. The player is vulnerable

The courier is not an action hero.

They can:
- be hurt;
- be robbed;
- be arrested;
- crash;
- fail contracts;
- starve after prolonged neglect;
- die.

Risk is meaningful because the player has a life worth returning to.

### 6. Small world, dense familiarity

A compact district is better than a giant empty map.

The player should begin to remember:
- the convenience store;
- the apartment;
- the parking garage;
- the locker;
- the shortcut;
- the dangerous courtyard;
- the usual police route;
- the girlfriend's location.

## Non-goals

Courier 404 is not:
- GTA-lite;
- a hacking simulator;
- a horror game built on cheap scares;
- a survival-bar maintenance game;
- a dating visual novel;
- a police simulator;
- a combat-first game;
- a courier-menu simulator.

## Long-term progression direction

The eventual full game may expand through:
- transport upgrades;
- better apartment/living conditions;
- relationship development;
- reputation with normal employers;
- reputation with anonymous clients;
- more districts;
- more contract types;
- higher stakes;
- deeper consequences.

These are future product directions, not permission to expand the overnight slice.
